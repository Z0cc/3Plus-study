高频句型 ​

首页

agree to sth.

同意某事

  1. ① I agree to the proposal(the plan). 我同意这个提议(计划)。
  2. ② Do you agree to this arrangement? 你赞成这个安排吗？

agree with sb.

同意某人(的意见等)

  1. ① I quite agree with what you said. 我很同意你说的。
  2. ② Almost everybody would agree with him. 几乎所有人都同意他的看法。

agree on sth.

就某事达成一致意见

  1. ① We couldn't agree on when to meet. 关于什么时候见面，我们未能取得一致。
  2. ② Agree on an agenda beforehand. 事先将议事日程达成一致。

as if/though

好像

  1. ① She ran as if the Old Boy were after her. 她跑得飞快好像魔鬼在追她似的。
  2. ② He acted as though he knew everything. 他表现得真像他什么都知道。

apologize to sb. for (doing) sth.

因(做了)某事向某人道歉

  1. ① You must apologize to him for your fault. 你必须为你的过失向他赔罪。
  2. ② She apologized to her teacher for coming to school late. 她因迟到向老师道歉。

as

引导的倒装句

  1. ① Child as she is, my daughter is not afraid to speak in public. 尽管我女儿还是个孩子，但她不怕在公众面前讲话。
  2. ② Tired as they were, they still kept on working. 虽然他们很累了，但是他们还是继续工作。

as...as...

像(和)……一样

  1. ① This story is as funny as that one. 这个故事和那个一样滑稽可笑。
  2. ② This book is not as necessary as they say. 这本书不想他们说的那么必不可少。

倍数 as...as...

是……的几倍

  1. ① Your car is twice as big as mine. 你的车是我的两倍大。
  2. ② This room is twice as large as that one. 这个房间是那个房间的两个大。

as/so long as

只要

  1. ① We'll go as long as the weather is good. 只要天气好我们就去。
  2. ② As long as you don't lose heart, you will succeed one day. 只要你不失去信心，你总有一天会成功的。

as soon as

一……就……

  1. ① As soon as I get my money I'm out of here! 我一旦拿到钱就走！
  2. ② Let us start as soon as he comes. 等他一来我们就动身。

ask/tell/advise/order/encourage sb. to do sth.

问/告诉/建议/命令/鼓励某人做某事

  1. ① She is tell to arrive at 8 o'clock on the dot. 她被告知八点钟准时到。
  2. ② I encourage them to talk all mutually. 我鼓励他们都互相交谈。

at the age of

在……岁的时候

  1. ① He was a middle school student at the age of 14. 他14岁时是个中学生。
  2. ② He became a doctor at the age of 27. 他27岁时成了一名医生。

be able to do sth.

能够做……

  1. ① She is able to speak three languages. 她能讲3种语言。
  2. ② He is able to drive the car. 他能开这个车。

be afraid of sth.

害怕某事

  1. ① Some students are afraid of exams. 有些学生害怕考试。
  2. ② Are you afraid of dogs? 你怕狗吗？

be busy doing sth.

忙于做某事

  1. ① He is busy doing his homework. 他正忙着做家庭作业。
  2. ② The buses are busy going and coming in the street. 街上公交车川流不息。

be busy with sth.

忙于某事

  1. ① He is busy with some important work. 他正忙于处理一些重要的事情。
  2. ② My mother is busy with the housework. 我妈妈忙着做家务。

be famous for

因……而著名

  1. ① He is famous for his learning. 他以有学问而出名。
  2. ② Beijing is famous for its ancient places of interest. 北京以其名胜古迹而著称。

be famous as

作为……而著名

  1. ① London is famous as a foggy city. 伦敦作为一个雾都而出名。
  2. ② Jin Yong is famous as a writer. 金庸是著名的作家。

be fond of (doing) sth.

喜欢(做)某事

  1. ① Are you fond of this kind of sports? 你喜欢这种运动吗？
  2. ② Trina professed to be fond of art. 屈丽娜自称爱好艺术。

be in charge of

管理，管辖

  1. ① I wanted to know who was in charge of the project. 我想知道是谁主管这个项目？
  2. ② He is in charge of the school work. 他负责学校工作。

be in the charge of sb.

归……管理，在……管辖下

  1. ① The project is in the charge of an experienced worker. 这项工程是由一位有经验的工人负责的。
  2. ② This company was in the charge of Mr Wang. 这家公司由王先生掌管。

be interested in (doing) sth.

对……感兴趣

  1. ① I am interested to know what happens. 我很想知道发生了什么事。
  2. ② We'd be interested to hear your story. 我们很想听听你的故事。

be late for sth.

做……迟到

  1. ① She hates to be late for work. 她不喜欢上班迟到。
  2. ② I will not be late for school. 我上学不会迟到。

be on duty

轮到，……值日

  1. ① It's time for you to be on duty. 轮到你值日的时间了。
  2. ② Who is on duty today? 今天谁值日？

be used to (doing) sth.

习惯(做)某事

  1. ① He is used to living alone. 他习惯一个人住。
  2. ② He is used to getting up early. 他习惯早起。

be used to do sth.

被用来做……

  1. ① Plastic is used to make bags. 塑料被用来制造塑料袋。
  2. ② Water is used to make drinks. 水被用来做饮料。

be worth doing sth.

值得做

  1. ① The movie is worth watching. 那部电影值得看。
  2. ② The new software is worth installing. 这个新软件值得安装。

be worthy of being done

值得做

  1. ① This issue is worthy of being studied carefully. 这一问题值得仔细研究。
  2. ② Guilin is worthy of being visited. 桂林值得去参观。

both...and...

两者都

  1. ① I can speak both English and Chinese. 我能讲中文和英文。
  2. ② They can both sing and dance. 他们都会唱歌和跳舞。

but引出的转折并列句

  1. ① The car is very old but it runs very fast. 虽然小汽车旧了，但跑得很快。
  2. ② He looks honest, but actually he's a rogue. 他看起来很老实，实际上他是一个坏蛋。

31. buy sb sth. /buy sth for sb.

给某人买……

  1. ① I am going to buy her a gift. 我要给她买一件礼物。
  2. ② I bought a bag for my mother. 我买了一个包给妈妈。

32. either...or...

要么……要么……；不是……就是……

  1. ① Either my father or my brothers are coming. 不是我父亲就是我兄弟要来。
  2. ② You can have either this one or that one. 你拿这个或那个都可以。

33. enough...to

够……做……

  1. ① He is old enough to drive a car. 他已经够大了，可以开车了。
  2. ② She ran fast enough to catch the bus. 她跑得够快，赶上了公交车。

34. had better do sth.

最好做某事

  1. ① Now you had better listen to the teacher. 你最好听老师讲。
  2. ② You had better be quiet. 你最好安静一些。

35. hardly/scarcely...when...

一……就……

  1. ① Scarcely had I reached there when it began to rain. 我一到那里天就开始下雨。
  2. ② Hardly had he arrived when she started complaining. 他刚一到，她就开始抱怨。

36. have no choice but to do sth.

除了……别无选择

  1. ① I have no choice but to believe what you're saying. 我除了相信你说的话别无选择。
  2. ② I have no choice but to accept his invitation. 我除了接受他的邀请没有别的选择。

37. hear sb. doing sth.

听见某人在做某事

  1. ① I heard someone singing when I passed by. 我经过这里时听到有人唱歌。
  2. ② I hear her crying. 我听到她在哭。

38. hear sb. do sth.

听见某人做了某事

  1. ① I like to hear you play the music. 我喜欢听你弹这首曲子。
  2. ② I often hear her sing. 我时常听她唱歌。

39. help sb. with sth./help sb. (to) do sth.

帮助……做……

  1. ① We helped her (to) find her things. 我们帮她找东西。
  2. ② He helps me with my Chinese. 他帮助我学中文。

40. how引导的感叹句

  1. ① How time flies! 时光飞逝！
  2. ② How fast he runs! 他跑得多快呀！

41. How/What about (doing) sth.?

(做)……怎么样？

  1. ① How about having a cup of tea? 喝杯茶怎么样？
  2. ② What about meeting the locals? 去和当地人接触呢？

42. if引导的条件状语从句

  1. ① He will come if you invite him. 如果你请他，他会来的。
  2. ② If I were you, I would never do that. 如果我是你，我决不做那种事。

43. in addition

而且，还有

  1. ① I committed an error in addition. 此外，我还犯了一个错误。
  2. ② You need money and time; in addition, you need diligence. 你需要钱和时间，而且还需要努力。

44. in case/in case of

万一

  1. ① In case of fire, you must keep calm and call 119. 假如遇上火警，你必须保持镇静并拨119火警。
  2. ② In case that it rains, you'd better take an umbrella. 以防下雨，你最好带上一把雨伞。

45. in order to do.../in order that...

为了……

  1. ① I wake up early in order to catch the first train. 我早起是为了赶上第一班火车。
  2. ② He worked hard in order that he could achieve his goals. 他努力工作，以便能够实现自己的目标。

46. in/on/to the+方位名词

  1. ① China is in the east of Asia. 中国在亚洲东部。
  2. ② Guangdong Province is on the southeast of Guangxi. 广东省在广西的东南边。
  3. ③ Japan is to the east of China. 日本在中国的东面。

47. 强调句 it is/was...that/who...

  1. ① It was Tom who(that) I met last week. 我上周遇见的就是汤姆。
  2. ② It is a new car that his brother wants to buy. 他的弟弟就是想买一辆新车。

48. It is good/bad manners to do sth.

做某事是(不)礼貌的。

  1. ① It is good manners to take a bunch of flowers to visit the host. 带一束花去拜访主人是有礼貌的表现。
  2. ② It is bad manners to talk with a full mouth. 嘴里吃着东西说话是不礼貌的。

49. It is important/necessary/possible/obvious for sb. to do sth.

某人做某事是重要的/必要的/可能的/明显的

  1. ① It is important for us to wear seat belts while driving cars. 开车时系安全带对我们很重要。
  2. ② It is necessary for us to have some exercise every day. 每天做些运动对我们来说是很有必要的。

50. It is no use/good/useless doing sth.

做某事是没用的。

  1. ① It is no good wasting time here. 在这里浪费时间毫无益处。
  2. ② It is useless talking too much. 说得太多了没有用处。

51. It is said/reported/predicted that...

据说/报道/预测……

  1. ① It is said that she lived to be over 100. 据说她活了100多岁。
  2. ② It is reported that another satellite has been sent into orbit in China this year. 据报道，今年中国又有一颗卫星被送入轨道。

52. It takes sb...to do sth.

花费某人……做某事

  1. ① It takes me about half an hour to get to the airport. 我到机场大概需要半小时。
  2. ② It takes me an hour to walk there and back. 我步行往返要花一个小时。

53. keep on doing sth.

继续做某事

  1. ① I'll keep on enjoying the movies. 我会继续欣赏电影。
  2. ② She had to keep on driving. 她不得不继续开车。

54. keep sb. doing sth.

让某人一直做某事

  1. ① He kept me waiting for half an hour. 他让我等了半个小时。
  2. ② I keep them waiting at the gate. 我让他们在大门口等候。

55. Let开头的两种反意疑问句

  1. ① Let's go, shall we? 咱们去吧，怎么样？
  2. ② Let us go, will you? 让我们去吧，行吗？

56. mind doing sth.

介意做某事

  1. ① He doesn't mind doing the dishes. 他不介意洗碗。
  2. ② Do you mind doing this with me? 你介意跟我一起做这些事吗？

57. more...than...

比……更……

  1. ① It was more expensive than I thought. 这比我预料的昂贵多了。
  2. ② There are many more people than we expected. 比我们想象的人要多得多。

58. more and more...

越来越……

  1. ① More and more people are giving up smoking. 戒烟的人越来越多了。
  2. ② The cranny in the table became more and more wide. 桌子上的裂缝越来越宽。

59. neither...nor...

既不……也不……

  1. ① I have neither time nor money. 我既无时间又无钱。
  2. ② I neither smoke nor drink. 我既不抽烟也不喝酒。

60. neither/nor+助动词/系动词/情态动词+主语表示否定的情况跟前者一样

  1. ① I don't like winter, neither/ nor does Jim. 我不喜欢冬天，吉姆也是。
  2. ② Mother has never been to Japan, neither has Father. 妈妈从未去过日本，爸爸也没去过。

61. no sooner...than

一……就

  1. ① He had no sooner gone to bed than his parents returned. 他刚躺下去睡觉，爸妈就回来了。
  2. ② He had no sooner returned than he bought a house. 他一回来就买了一套房子。

62. not...but...

不是……而是……

  1. ① He wants to buy not a skirt, but a dress. 他想买的不是一条短裙，而是一条连衣裙。
  2. ② Miss Cai is not a writer, but a teacher. 蔡老师不是一名作家，而是一位老师。

63. not...until...

直到……才……

  1. ① We didn't arrive at our hotel until after midnight. 我们直到午夜后才到达旅馆。
  2. ② Don't get off the bus until it stops. 在车停下来之前不要下车。

64. not only...but also...

不但……而且……

  1. ① She not only wrote the text but also selected the illustrations. 她不仅写了正文部分，而且还挑选了插图。
  2. ② It not only saved money, but also saved time. 不仅节省了金钱，还节省了时间。

65. on account of/because of

因为……，由于……

  1. ① The flight was postponed on account of bad weather. 因为天气恶劣，飞行延期了。
  2. ② She resigned because of ill health. 她因健康状况不佳而辞职。

66. on one's way to...

在去……的路上

  1. ① It was on the way to the station. 那是在去火车站的路上。
  2. ② She is on her way to school. 她在去上学的路上。

67. one of + 名词的复数

……之一

  1. ① It was one of my many mistakes. 这是我犯的许多错误中的一个。
  2. ② He's one of my best friends. 他是我最好的朋友之一。

68. or 引导的选择并列句

  1. ① Take a vacation, or you'll get sick. 你休假吧，不然会生病的。
  2. ② Hurry up, or you will be late. 快点，不然你会迟到。

69. prefer...to...

宁愿……而不愿……

  1. ① I prefer reading a newspaper to watching TV. 比起看电视，我宁愿看报纸。
  2. ② I prefer cooking myself to eating out. 我宁愿自己做饭也不愿出去吃。

70. sb. pay...for sth.

某人付钱买某物

  1. ① I paid twenty dollars for the book. 我花了二十美元买那本书。
  2. ② He paid 500 dollars for his rent. 他付了500美元付房租。

71. see sb. do sth.

看见某人做过某事

  1. ① I see her coming into the station. 我看见她走进车站。
  2. ② I saw her cross the street. 我看到她过马路。

72. see sb. doing sth.

看见某人在做某事

  1. ① I saw her reading the book. 我看到她正在读书。
  2. ② I saw you playing the game. 我看到你正在玩游戏。

73. since引导的时间状语从句

  1. ① He's been there ever since you left. 自从你离开，他一直都在那儿。
  2. ② Cath hasn't phoned since she went to Berlin. 卡思自从去了柏林还没有打来过电话。

74. so+助动词/系动词/情态动词+主语表示后者也和前者一样

  1. ① He likes English, so do I. 他喜欢英语，我也是。
  2. ② I play computer well, so does he. 我计算机玩得好，他也是。

75. so...that

如此……以至于

  1. ① This teacher is so kind that we all like him. 这个老师非常和蔼，我们都喜欢他。
  2. ② This is so interesting a book that we all enjoy reading it. 这本书非常有趣，我们都喜欢看。

76. so that引导的目的状语从句

“以便”

  1. ① She worked hard so that everything would be ready in time. 她卖力地干着，为的是一切能够及时就绪。
  2. ② I'll give you a key so that you can let yourself in. 我把钥匙给你，你可以自己开门进去。

77. 形容词修饰不定代词要倒装

  1. ① I have something important to tell you. 我有重要的事情要告诉你。
  2. ② Colin knew something new was coming. 科林知道有什么新鲜事要发生了。

78. spend...on sth./(in)doing sth.

花费……做某事

  1. ① She spent £100 on a new dress. 她花100英镑买了一条新连衣裙。
  2. ② He spends his whole life (in) looking after the poor. 他一生都在照顾穷人。

79. sth. cost sb. ...

某物花了……

  1. ① Tickets cost ten dollars each. 每张票价为十元。
  2. ② Matches cost only a few pence. 火柴只需几便士。

80. sth. need/want/require doing/to be done

  1. ① The cut will need to be stitched. 这伤口需要缝合。
  2. ② These young seedlings will require looking after carefully. 这些幼苗需要细心照料。

81. thank sb. for(doing)sth.

  1. ① I must thank you for being so kind to me. 真要感谢您对我这么好。
  2. ② We thank you for your attention! 感谢您的关注！

82. the first to do sth.

第一个做某事

  1. ① Sheila and Jim were the first to arrive. 希拉和吉姆是最先到的。
  2. ② He'd always be the first to offer to help. 他总是第一个主动提出帮忙。

83. the more..., the more...

越……就越……

  1. ① The more you pay, the more you get. 付出越多，收获越多。
  2. ② The older you get, the less sleep you will need. 年龄越大，所需睡眠就越少。

84. There be/stand/live/lie

有……

  1. ① There is no viable alternative. 没有其他可行的措施。
  2. ② There stands a building. 那有一座楼宇。

85. think/find/make it + 补语, it作形式宾语

  1. ① I think it highly unlikely that I'll get the job. 我认为我得到这份工作的可能性极小。
  2. ② I find it absolutely astonishing that you didn't like it. 你不喜欢它，我感到难以置信。

86. though/although引导的让步状语从句

  1. ① Although the sun was shining it wasn't very warm. 尽管太阳高照，却不很暖和。
  2. ② Though she was tiny, she had a very loud voice. 虽然她个头很小，嗓门却很大。

87. too...to...

太……而不能……

  1. ① He is too young to look after himself. 他太小了，还没办法照顾自己。
  2. ② He is too shy to give a speech. 他太害羞，不敢做演讲。

88. unless引导的条件状语从句

除非……

  1. ① Training is worthless unless there is proof that it works. 除非能证明训练有效，否则做就是白搭。
  2. ② They won't come unless you say sorry. 除非你道歉，要不然他们不来。

89. used to do sth.

过去常常做某事

  1. ① I used to play dominoes there. 我过去常在那里玩多米诺骨牌游戏。
  2. ② I used to play hero as a boy. 我小时候常在这里玩。

90. what引导的感叹句

  1. ① What bloody awful weather! 多么糟糕透顶的天气！
  2. ② What beautiful flowers they are! 多么美丽的花啊！

91. when引导的时间状语从句

  1. ① Call me when you've finished. 你完成后就打电话给我。
  2. ② Your mother will blame you when she sees this mess. 当你母亲看到这凌乱的局面时她会责骂你的。

92. work sth. out/work out sth.

算出……

  1. ① I need to work out a plan for my project. 我需要制定一个项目计划。
  2. ② I can't work it out in my head - I need a calculator. 我没法心算出来——我得用计算器。

93. would rather...than.../would...rather than...

宁愿……也不愿……

  1. ① They would rather work than live on welfare. 他们宁愿工作而不愿靠社会保障金过活。
  2. ② Facing the enemies, our soldiers would die rather than surrender. 面对敌人，我们的战士宁死不屈。

94. 表语从句

  1. ① That's why I can't go with you. 那就是我不能同你一起去的原因。
  2. ② The problem is how we can find him. 问题是我们如何找到他。

95. 宾语从句

  1. ① Could you tell me who knows the answer, please? 你能告诉我谁知道答案吗？
  2. ② I worry about whether I hurt her feelings. 我担心是否伤了她的感情。

96. 定语从句

  1. ① He is a man who never breaks his word. 他是个从不食言的人。
  2. ② This is the best film that I have ever seen. 这部电影是我看过的最好的一部。

97. 反义疑问句

  1. ① They are very late for the meeting, aren't they? 他们开会迟到了，是吗？
  2. ② You come from Beijing, don't you? 你来自北京，是不是？

98. 祈使句

  1. ① Shut the door, please. 请关门。
  2. ② Come in and sit down. 进来坐。

99. 同位语从句

  1. ① I have no idea whether he'll come or not. 我不知道他是否来。
  2. ② It is a question how he did it. 那是一个他如何做了此事的问题。

100. 主语从句

  1. ① That we shall be late is certain. 我们要晚了，这是确定无疑的。
  2. ② That he should have ignored the working class was natural. 他忽视工人阶级是极其自然的。
